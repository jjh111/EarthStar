# Research Veracity — the Earth Star Layer

The generic protocol (claim taxonomy, source ladder, verification loop, ledger, numbers
discipline, attribution, failure modes) is `../SKILL.md` and is not repeated here. This
file carries what is **specific to Earth Star work**: why the framework needs the protocol
more than most, how its five registers and three voices map onto the taxonomy, how to
attribute its historical spine, and the anti-patterns it is prone to. Load it when the
`earth-star` skill is also loaded and the deliverable is fact-bearing.

## Why Earth Star needs this more than most

Earth Star speaks with certainty — "we make it," "10,000 years of proof," "reconnaissance
from the future." That certainty is the framework's soul: a chosen stance, a commitment
device, the accretion disk of hope. It is not evidence, and it must never be handled as
evidence.

An agent that inherits the mythic register and applies it to research work will cite
visions as facts, inflate confidence, and skip verification. That output then fails the
very people the framework exists to serve — the policy staffer who cites it, the skeptical
engineer who fact-checks it, the community that acts on it. **Unverified claims are
informational waste. The protocol metabolizes them.** Truth-tending is tending: the same
care given to a beach is given to a claim.

## The taxonomy, with Earth Star's own examples

| Tag | Earth Star examples | Handling |
|-----|---------------------|----------|
| `[E]` | Microplastics detected in human blood (Leslie et al. 2022); US MSW tonnage (EPA); live solar-wind Bz (SWPC, with fetch time) | cite with date; numbers from the original measurement |
| `[H]` | California indigenous land management (Anderson, *Tending the Wild*, 2005); pre-contact wealth-spreading customs | attribute source **and** peoples |
| `[D]` | Gomen specifications; diamond windows; reversible computing at scale; LEO debris agents; the Viewer's Shue-1998 shield | "designed to," "would," "targets" — never "does" |
| `[P]` | The Waste Reclamation Fund Act; the Harmonious Computing Act | state status: model bill, not filed, not enacted |
| `[M]` | The Forest City dream; "we make it"; the butterfly in the cocoon of empire | opens, closes, motivates — never testifies |

**The register rule, in Earth Star's words:** *certainty is a spiritual stance, not an
evidentiary one.* The dream stays. The dream is never a citation.

## Three voices → one protocol

- **Mythic** — the protocol still runs, invisibly: a manifesto may not misstate an `[E]`
  fact even while it sings. Tag in the working copy; strip tags in the final.
- **Bridge** — the protocol is the argument. Lead with the audience's values and `[E]`/`[H]`
  material; the ledger is what lets the piece survive the skeptic's first question.
- **Analytic** — tags or equivalent inline signals *stay visible*; the ledger ships as an
  appendix or sidecar file.

Most Earth Star deliverables braid registers — a manifesto opening, an analytic core, a
visionary close. That braid is the house style and it works **only when the seams are
honest**: shifts felt or marked; the analytic core standing alone with every `[M]`
sentence removed.

## Attribution of the historical spine

The framework's historical spine is California indigenous land stewardship. Handle it as
a living inheritance, not free material:

- Cite the documented source: M. Kat Anderson, *Tending the Wild: Native American
  Knowledge and the Management of California's Natural Resources* (University of
  California Press, 2005) — itself a synthesis of tribal knowledge-holders' testimony.
- Name specific peoples and nations where the source names them, rather than flattening
  to "indigenous wisdom."
- Distinguish *documented historical practice* `[H]` from *Earth Star's translation of it*
  `[D]` — the mapping of fire ecology onto "metabolize disorder" is the framework's move,
  not the tribes' claim. The kincentric term traces to Enrique Salmón (2000, *Ecological
  Applications* 10(5)) — cite him when using it.
- Never speak *for* living indigenous communities, present them as endorsing Earth Star,
  or imply the framework is itself indigenous. It is a re-encoding that owes and
  acknowledges its debt.

## ∇(𝒞) — the numbers rule the framework most needs

∇(𝒞) is a direction, not yet a unit. **It is never quantified by invention.** Where a real
proxy exists (soil organic matter, species counts, circularity rates, biomarkers, tracked
debris counts, measured Kp), cite the proxy and its measurement. Where none exists, say so
and — in Earth Star fashion — propose what the measurement would be. A made-up coherence
value is coherence theater, and it composts trust.

## Earth Star's own anti-patterns (the ones this file exists to catch)

From the core skill's failure-mode list, the epistemic ones:

1. **Mythos leakage** — "the dream confirms," "proof from the future" in a policy memo.
2. **Certainty inflation** — the vow adjusting a cost estimate, a timeline, a feasibility claim.
3. **Coherence theater** — invented ∇(𝒞) scores.
4. **Appropriation drift** — the framework presenting borrowed knowledge as its own.
5. **Seven-generation vagueness** — deep time used to avoid a first-generation number.

Each is caught by the generic loop in `../SKILL.md`; each is *named* here because the
framework's voice makes them easy to commit without noticing.
