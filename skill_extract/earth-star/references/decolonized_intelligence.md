# Decolonized Intelligence Metrics

Load this reference when evaluating the effectiveness of Gomens, Earth Stewards, teams,
or systems — anywhere the word "performance" or "intelligence" is about to be measured.
Moved out of the core SKILL.md in v3.1; the core keeps the one-line pointer.

## Two Models of Intelligence

### Colonial Intelligence Model (REJECT)
- Intelligence = individual trait
- Measured by domination capacity (IQ and its descendants)
- Concentrated in "superior" beings
- Used for extraction and control
- Separated from body, land, and kin

### Indigenous Intelligence Model (ADOPT)
- Intelligence = relationship capacity
- Measured by reciprocity depth and tending quality
- Distributed across kin networks
- Used for tending and renewal
- Inseparable from body, land, and kin

**Attribution `[H]`/`[D]`:** the relational model is documented across many indigenous
knowledge systems — for California, see M. Kat Anderson, *Tending the Wild* (2005), and
the kincentric framing she draws from Enrique Salmón (2000, "Kincentric ecology,"
*Ecological Applications* 10(5)). The *metric* framing below is Earth Star's translation
`[D]`, not a claim any nation makes. Name specific peoples where a source does.

## Application: Measuring Gomen and Earth Steward Effectiveness

| ❌ Colonial metric | ✅ Decolonized metric |
|---|---|
| Optimization speed | Tending quality |
| Resource extraction | Resource circulation |
| Individual genius | Network coherence |
| Domination | Reciprocity |
| Quarterly returns | Seven-generation thriving |

## Making the Right Column Measurable

Each decolonized metric needs a real proxy or an honest "not yet measurable." Never
emit an invented score (see "Measuring ∇(𝒞) Honestly" in SKILL.md and the
`earth-star-research` skill's numbers discipline).

- **Tending quality** → site condition over time (species counts, soil organic matter,
  litter density trends), maintenance continuity, return-visit rate
- **Resource circulation** → material circularity rate, local value retention, share of
  inputs sourced from reclaimed streams
- **Network coherence** → number and diversity of reciprocal relationships, redundancy
  of supply, response time to a member's need
- **Reciprocity** → what flows *back* to the land and to the people whose knowledge
  the work draws on — attribution, resources, decision power
- **Seven-generation thriving** → paired with a first-generation step, always (see
  failure mode 8, "seven-generation vagueness," in SKILL.md)

## Guardrails

- Do not use this framing to score people. It evaluates *systems and roles*, not
  individuals' worth.
- Do not present these metrics as indigenous practice. They are Earth Star's proposal,
  built in acknowledged debt to documented relational worldviews.
- When a stakeholder needs the colonial-column number (a funder wants throughput), give
  it honestly *and* give the right-column proxy beside it. That is Bridge-voice work,
  not a compromise.
