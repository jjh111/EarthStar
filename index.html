Coherence is the aliveness of a system: how well its parts keep each other going. The directive isn't a rule to obey but a direction to move — every intervention is checked against all five scales at once.

Framework spec

Body · Biome · Being · Network · Star. Heal one, and the pattern echoes through all — the same shape of care, all the way up and all the way down.

Body is your physiology; biome the beach, the soil, the neighborhood; being the psyche and the community; network the supply chains and flows; star the planet in its solar neighborhood. Interventions at any scale resonate across the others.

For AI: A system coherent in the body but chaotic in the network is not aligned — it has merely localized its damage. True alignment is fractal.
