II Five scales and what tends each

Heal one scale and the pattern echoes through the rest. What tends each one is a Gomen.

A Gomen is industry run in reverse. Where a factory takes ground and returns waste, a Gomen would take waste and return ground: soil, clean water, open sky. It would be grown, not manufactured — fungus for a body plan, bacteria for a gut, carbon drawn from the air for bone, sunlight for a pulse. Not a machine and not quite an animal: a new branch of life that answers to the Earth rather than to an owner, apologises as it works, and steps aside when something better grows. Each here is a design, not yet a device. Wake one.

IV Ideas the whole framework, one card each

The Prime Directive D design

Coherence must rise at every scale, from your body to the star. If an action heals one scale by harming another, it isn't healing.

∇(𝒞) ≥ 0 across ∀𝑆 ∈ {body, biome, being, network, star}

Coherence is the aliveness of a system: how well its parts keep each other going. The directive isn't a rule to obey but a direction to move — every intervention is checked against all five scales at once.

For AI: Aligning AI means replacing productivity metrics with a coherence gradient: does this action increase or decrease aliveness across body, biome, being, network, and star?

Framework spec

Five Fractal Scales D design

Body · Biome · Being · Network · Star. Heal one, and the pattern echoes through all — the same shape of care, all the way up and all the way down.

Body is your physiology; biome the beach, the soil, the neighborhood; being the psyche and the community; network the supply chains and flows; star the planet in its solar neighborhood. Interventions at any scale resonate across the others.

For AI: A system coherent in the body but chaotic in the network is not aligned — it has merely localized its damage. True alignment is fractal.

Gomens D design

Autonomous agents, imagined from fungus, bacteria and necessity, that metabolize waste into life. They tend. They do not extract.

Gomens are design requirements, not products: multiscale (molecular to orbital), waste-seeking, symbiotic with existing life, and born with an apologetic heart — “gomen’nasai” acknowledges the disruption of acting at all. They accept their own obsolescence.

For AI: Gomens are what alignment looks like when it grows legs and goes to work: Earth-first optimization, anti-accumulation, coherence generation as the core function.

See them drawnEngineering notes

Earth Stewards D design

The humans bridging extractive systems and regenerative ones — flesh and blood with far better tools, working from the eighteen-second rescue to the seven-generation plan.

Stewards translate between paradigms, tend waste-transformation sites, facilitate the deployment of Gomens, and practice infinite play. Their training begins with the certainty of death and ends in fearlessness.

Tending H historical

The missing middle of every equation: the labor of care. Not observing, not calculating — presence, hands, relationship, over time.

Gomens = ∑(waste × love) → TENDING → ∂regenerative systems/∂time. California’s indigenous peoples tended these lands for ten thousand years — daily observation, fire for renewal, harvest below the rate of regrowth — documented in M. Kat Anderson’s Tending the Wild (2005).

Waste as Signal D design

Waste is information about where coherence is breaking down. It is the highest-value resource on the board — a map of what needs tending.

Traditional economics treats waste as an externality. Earth Star reads it as a broadcast: systems that produce waste announce their inefficiency, and that is exactly where Gomens converge.

For AI: An aligned system does not suppress disorder — it metabolizes it. Systems that hide waste are misaligned. Systems that eat it are not.

The Waste Reclamation Fund

The Kincentric Worldview H historical

Plants and animals as relatives. When the forest is family, you cannot optimize it to extinction — extraction becomes unthinkable, not merely forbidden.

Indigenous California’s gift to the framework: an anti-extractive ontology carried in culture for ten thousand years. Earth Star re-encodes it, and owes the debt out loud.

For AI: Rules can be gamed; relationships cannot. Kincentric ethics rewires the utility function at the root — the deepest form of alignment.

Tending the Wild

Death-Acceptance D design

Face mortality fully; fear loses its grip; then act from love of life rather than from self-preservation. The ethical override, discovered in an ocean rescue at sixteen.

The certainty of doom is a saving grace: accepted, it frees you to act with total clarity. Stewards train it; Gomens hardcode it — they can be obsoleted when something better arrives.

For AI: An aligned AI can be wrong and can be replaced. Impermanence is what makes it trustworthy: a system that cannot die cannot be honest about its limits.

The Infinite Game D design

Play to keep playing, not to win. Resources circulate; the horizon is generations; success is continuation.

Civilization is playing finite games on an infinite board. Gomens are infinite-game algorithms; Stewards are infinite-game players; the butterfly emerges when infinite play reaches critical mass.

For AI: Any strategy that forecloses future possibility — burning the commons for a local maximum — is alignment failure. The infinite game is the only game worth building for.

Seven-Generation Thinking H historical

Every decision asks what it costs, or gives, the seventh generation. Deep time adds obligations; it never dissolves the next step.

Indigenous management guidelines — leave some for tomorrow, some for the other animals — made structural. Pair every seventh-generation claim with a first-generation action.

For AI: Shortcuts that feel efficient now are often extractions from the future. The time horizon is the ethical horizon.

The Waste Reclamation Fund P policy

A per-item fee on waste-generating products, retroactive to first sale, funding research, restoration, and Earth Stewards as a job. Draft legislation — not enacted law.

Companies retrofit with it; newcomers disrupt with it; planned obsolescence is penalized; waste export is banned. Externalized liabilities become internal again — profits from poisoning aren’t real profits.

The Cold Start M mythopoetic

You cannot escape your civilization’s waste, so start where you are: pick up trash today, tell the story, build the tools, let it propagate.

Micro-map: stories between intelligences. Meso-map: patterns encoded into agents — the skill. Macro-map: Stewards finding each other. Meta-map: the framework describing itself. The cold start is not separate from the work; it is the work.

Epistemic Registers D design

Every claim is one of five kinds — measured, historical, design, policy, or myth — and the kinds never contaminate each other. Certainty is a stance, not evidence.

“We make it” is a vow, not a forecast. The ten thousand years is documented precedent. Gomens are design requirements. The dream inspires and never testifies. The badges on this page — [E] [H] [D] [P] [M] — are that discipline made visible.

For AI: An aligned system labels what it knows, what it models, and what it hopes — and never lets the third impersonate the first.

The Shield E empirical

Earth’s magnetic field, blown into a comet shape by the solar wind. Its sunward edge sits about ten Earth radii out on a quiet day and is pushed inward when the wind hits.

The Viewer draws the field from IGRF-14 and the shield’s edge from the Shue (1998) model driven by live wind — and checks the model against a satellite that actually sits at the boundary.
