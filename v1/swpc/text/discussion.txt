Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity reached moderate levels. Region 4551 (S15W25,
Eki/beta-gamma-delta) produced an impulsive M1.3/1n flare (R1-Minor) at
16/1542 UTC, the strongest of the period. This region along with Region
4557 (N17E07, Dai/beta) produced most of the C-class X-ray activity
observed in the past 24 hours. These two regions exhibited minor growth
and development among their spots, with the former developing relatively
small mixed polarity spots inside its large, leading penumbra. Region
4555 (S07W78, Axx/alpha) appeared to be in decay as it began its transit
of the western limb.

A notable CME was first observed by LASCO C2 coronagraph imagery at
approximately 17/0236 UTC. This event originated from prominence
eruption just on or behind the eastern limb, as evidenced by GOES SUVI
304 imagery beginning near 17/0125 UTC, and is thus not Earth-directed.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at low levels through 19 Oct, with a
slight chance (20%) for M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) due
mainly to the flare potential of Region 4551.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a peak
flux of 1,470 pfu at 16/1450 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux
was at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected be at normal to
moderate levels through 19 Oct with a chance for brief periods of high
levels. No S1 (Minor) solar radiation storms are expected through 19
Oct.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters were slightly elevated under diminishing HSS
activity. Total field (Bt) maintained near 4-6 nT, with a variable Bz
(north-south) component that was near neutral or northward. Solar wind
speeds underwent a steady decrease, ending the reporting period near 420
km/s. The phi angle was predominantly negative while undertaking several
excursions into a positive orientation.

.Forecast...
Solar wind conditions are expected to trend towards nominal levels as
influences from a negative polarity CH HSS slowly wanes, with a return
to near-background/ambient levels by 18 Oct. Nominal conditions are
expected to then persist through 19 Oct.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at quiet to active levels.

.Forecast...
Mostly quiet conditions with isolated unsettled periods are expected on
17 Oct under diminishing CH HSS effects. Quiet conditions are expected
to prevail on 18 Oct and continue through 19 Oct.
