Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was low due to weak C-class flare activity observed from
Region 4524 (N12W27, Hsx/alpha), the largest a C1.6/Sf at 10/1712 UTC.
B-class flare activity was observed from Region 4521 (N09W67, Hsx/alpha)
and 4525 (N11W10, Cri/beta). Both Regions 4525 and 4530 (N19W21,
Dri/beta) exhibited some intermediate spot growth. The remaining regions
were either stable or in decay.

Other activity included a faint CME observed off the western limb at
about 09/1900 UTC in STEREO A and CCOR imagery from a potential filament
liftoff near center disk. Another filament liftoff was observed near
S15W30 at about 10/0315 UTC with a more obvious CME off the western
limb. Both CMEs were modelled as slow-moving with potential weak Earth
impact late on the 14th into the 15th.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is anticipated to remain low, with a slight chance of
low-level M-class flares (R1-Minor) 11-13 Sep. There is little evidence
in NSO GONG helioseismology of any looming active regions of note just
beyond the east limb.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels with a maximum
flux of 3,077 pfu observed at 10/1745 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux was at background values.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels
11-12 Sep and decline to low to moderate on 13 Sep. The greater than 10
MeV proton flux is expected to remain at background levels.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind conditions were at mostly background levels. Total IMF
strength peaked at 6 nT, while the Bz component varied between +3 to -4
nT. Solar wind speed changes were minimal, varying between 417-486 km/s
The phi angle was mostly in a negative orientation from 10/0000-10/1015
UTC, and predominantly positive through the end of the period.

.Forecast...
Solar wind conditions are anticipated to experience some minor
disturbances due mainly to a small, positive polarity CH HSS
anticipated to be geoeffective through 12 Sep. Negative CH HSS effects
are anticipated to begin on 13 Sep.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was at quiet to unsettled levels.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is expected to be quiet to unsettled through 13
Sep with active periods possible on 11 Sep due to CH HSS onset.
