Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at moderate levels. Region 4547 (N15, L=089) produced
an M1.3 flare (R1-Minor radio blackouts) at 09/1409 UTC. This region has
been off the visible disk for well over 24 hours, so the associated CME,
first visible in LASCO C2 at 09/1436 UTC, is not anticipated to have an
impact at Earth. The region produced a C4.2 at 10/0813 UTC that GOES
SUVI imagery suggests is also associated with an eruption, though no
complementary structure has been observed in coronagraph imagery due to
a data gap; this eruption is also anticipated to have no Earth impact
due the farsided location of the source region.

Region 4549 (N09W54, Eki/beta-delta) added multiple C-class flares, the
largest being a C7.9 at 10/0438 UTC. This region exhibited decay in its
trailer spots, but continued to experience growth in its leading
cluster. Region 4545 (N17W60, Cro/beta) continued to decay with no
activity noted during the period. Region 4548 (S13W26, Hsx/alpha)
remained stable and inactive, while Region 4552 (N22W34, Bxo/beta) began
to simplify. Region 4551 (S14E61, Eki/beta) continued to move into view
and produced a few small C-flares.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to be at low levels (90%) with M-class flares
(R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) likely (55%) through 12 Oct, primarily due to
the flaring potential of Region 4549.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux was at moderate levels. The greater
than 10 MeV proton flux remained slightly elevated above background
levels but still well below the alert threshold.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach normal to
moderate levels through 11 Oct before increasing to high levels on 12
Oct. There is a slight chance (10%) for a greater than 10 MeV proton
event exceeding the 10 pfu (S1-Minor) threshold through 12 Oct.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters were disturbed this reporting period, likely a
combination of weak glancing transient and negative polarity coronal
hole high speed stream influences. After 10/0200 UTC, solar wind speed
increased from an average of 375 km/s to an average of 410 km/s. Total
field strength ranged from 2 to 11 nT, with a Bz component consistently
weakly southward after approximately 09/1620 UTC, with a short-lived max
deflection of -7 nT at 10/0622 UTC. The phi angle slowly oscillated
between sectors over the course of the reporting period.

.Forecast...
Additional enhancements are likely on 10 Oct as a negative polarity
coronal hole high speed stream continues to move into a geoeffective
position. Stronger disturbances are anticipated on 11 Oct due to CMEs
that left the Sun on 07-09 Oct.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field was quiet to unsettled.

.Forecast...
Unsettled to active conditions are expected, with isolated G1 (Minor)
storm levels likely, through 10 Oct as a negative polarity coronal hole
high speed stream becomes geoeffective. G1-G2 conditions are again
anticipated on 11 Oct due to persistent CH HSS effects combined with
additional CME arrivals that left the Sun on 07-09 Oct.
