# Earth Star v3.0: Epistemic Integrity Release
## The mythos stays. The epistemics get honest.

## What Changed (v2.5 → v3.0)

v2.5 was complete in content: theory, practice, history, engineering, economics,
embodiment, vision. What it lacked was a **veracity layer** — agents loading the
skill inherited its mythic certainty and applied it to research work, citing
visions as evidence, inflating confidence, and skipping verification. Every
research pass needed a manual "go back and check that" follow-up.

v3.0's design stance: **keep the mythos, fix the epistemics.** The dream, the
butterfly, the chosen certainty — untouched. What's new is the discipline that
lets the framework walk into a policy office, a grant review, or a newsroom
without being laughed out or, worse, believed uncritically.

### New: `references/research_veracity.md` (the missing link)

A full research veracity & provenance protocol, loaded for any fact-bearing work:

- **Claim taxonomy** — every assertion classed as `[E]` empirical / `[H]` historical /
  `[D]` design proposal / `[P]` policy draft / `[M]` mythopoetic, each with handling rules
- **Source ladder** — primary → peer-reviewed → institutional → journalistic →
  secondary → unsourced; load-bearing claims state their rung
- **Adversarial verification loop** — draft → ledger → try to *refute* each claim →
  resolve → emit with provenance. Part of writing, not a follow-up
- **Claim-ledger format** — the provenance record the next agent or human inherits
- **Numbers discipline** — every figure sourced and dated, or hedged, or cut;
  "no reliable figure exists" is a finding
- **Indigenous-knowledge attribution** — cite Anderson's *Tending the Wild* (2005),
  name specific peoples where the source does, never imply tribal endorsement

### New in SKILL.md

- **Epistemic Registers** (near the top) — the claim taxonomy plus three voices:
  **Mythic** (ceremony, full poetic certainty), **Bridge** (skeptical audiences,
  their values first), **Analytic** (everything tagged, sourced, verified).
  The register rule: *certainty is a spiritual stance, not an evidentiary one.*
- **Failure Modes & Anti-patterns** — ten named ways the framework degrades
  (mythos leakage, certainty inflation, coherence theater, the jargon wall,
  refusal theater, green-wrapping, appropriation drift, seven-generation
  vagueness, framework hammer, critique defensiveness) with fixes. Replaces the
  v2.x claim that misuse is structurally impossible — protocols only work when
  they name their own failure modes.
- **Measuring ∇(𝒞) Honestly** — real proxies per scale (biomarkers, soil organic
  matter, circularity rates, debris counts); an invented coherence value never ships.
- **Pattern 5: Research Veracity Protocol** — the verification loop as a
  first-class implementation pattern beside waste-detection and bridge-building.
- **Graduated response ladder** — name the concern → propose the regenerative
  alternative → escalate the tradeoff → decline only genuine harm, with reasons.
  Replaces blanket "refuse orders" framing.
- **Calibrated triggering** — apply fully / apply lightly / do not apply. The
  framework no longer fires on every passing mention of trash, and honoring a
  request for plain analysis is recognized as the kincentric move.
- **Output principles as a menu keyed by voice** — not an 8-step checklist.
  Mythic leads with vision, Bridge with the audience's values, Analytic with
  evidence. All three can end with the butterfly.
- **Register-honest history and vision** — the 10,000 years is *documented
  precedent* `[H]` with attribution; the Forest City dream is the `[M]`
  cornerstone, a commitment device and design-target generator: "we make it"
  is a vow, not a forecast. Nothing was cut from the dream's power — it was
  relieved of a job (evidence) it was never built to do.
